## Software people can maintain

I'm Alejandro, also known as Jano. I have spent more than 15 years building software across architecture, backend engineering, infrastructure, and technical leadership. I care most about systems that survive their first deployment: clear boundaries, observable operations, and decisions a team can explain and sustain.

My path includes production platforms, systems for regional organizations, local infrastructure, integrations, and developer tools. I prefer to explain that work through problems, constraints, and decisions rather than a list of technologies without context.

## Tools and community

Open source is an important part of how I learn and share. I maintain projects around tmux, Unix, automation, and reproducible environments, and I write about terminals, Linux, and everyday engineering practice.

## Beyond work

I live in Bolívar, Buenos Aires Province. I'm a minimalist at heart, an eclectic reader and listener, and a harmonica learner. I enjoy cooking for family and friends, especially Italian pasta, and I prefer walking or cycling whenever I can.

You can explore my work on [GitHub](https://github.com/janoamaral) or get in touch through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/janoamaral/).
